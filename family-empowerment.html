Parent Power/Family Empowerment

Families are essential partners in the educational journey; EIEI strengthens the support system around the child, fostering trust and cooperation among all stakeholders. EIEI’s journey in special education is one of dedication and compassion. By humanizing the approach to teaching and learning, we are shaping a future where every child can succeed and thrive. EIEI has become a beacon of hope for children with varying abilities and disabilities, empowering families to work hand in hand toward a brighter and more inclusive tomorrow.

EIEI helps parents and caregivers of children and young people with disabilities, and special healthcare needs to develop an understanding of healthcare, education, and disability policies. We empower families to increase their impact and voice to promote change beneficial to children, young people, and families.

Resource Bank

In 2020, KenCrest launched the SWIFT Support Program, an innovative early childhood behavioral intervention designed to help children with challenging behaviors remain successfully enrolled in preschools and early learning centers. This program delivers behavioral support for children at risk of preschool expulsion, ensuring they thrive in inclusive classrooms.

Learn more at KenCrest SWIFT Support Services .

Disability Advice empowers individuals throughout every step of the Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) claims process. Their team offers free case evaluations and guidance for families navigating an often overwhelming system.

Explore their guide, including Parenting a Child with a Disability, and other tools to support your family.

Access valuable autism resources, including Autism Levels: Why They Matter and Why the Criticism Misses the Point, to deepen your understanding of autism spectrum differences and support your child’s unique strengths.

Elwyn’s Early Learning Services offers Preschool Early Intervention programs for children aged three to school entry with developmental delays and disabilities in Philadelphia County and the city of Chester. Each year, ELS supports approximately 10,000 children, promoting development and enhancing family and community capacity.

Families can contact the Access to Care line at 215-222-8054 or visit Elwyn’s Early Learning Services for more information and regional contact details.

The Pennsylvania State Interagency Coordinating Council (SICC) advises state departments to ensure a comprehensive, coordinated Early Intervention system for infants, toddlers, young children, and their families. The portal includes information about vision, meeting schedules, membership, and resources.

Visit the Early Intervention Technical Assistance Portal at eita-pa.org.

Access a downloadable list of Local Interagency Coordinating Councils (LICCs) to find collaborative teams supporting Early Intervention efforts within communities across Pennsylvania.

Learn how Montessori principles can make parenting more fulfilling by fostering independence, focus, and self-regulation. Resources include guides on setting up the home environment, supporting independent play, and building your child’s confidence and patience.

Explore articles and guides from trusted Montessori organizations to bring these strategies into your home.

McAndrews Law Offices provide families in Pennsylvania with legal representation in special education, estate planning, and disability law. Their team supports families in understanding and securing their legal rights and protections.

Access instructions for taking and scoring the Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised (M-CHAT-R), along with resources connected to World Autism Month to support early identification and intervention.

As You Are is a virtual clinic with physicians specializing in autism evaluations for children 16 months to 10 years (and re-evaluations for ages 11–17). No referral is required, and most insurance plans are accepted.

Families can discuss developmental concerns, explore whether a diagnostic assessment is needed, and receive guidance and resources for next steps.

A toolkit designed to strengthen collaboration between families, schools, and communities. When adults come together to support children, profound changes can happen. This resource offers practical strategies and tools to deepen engagement.

PaTTAN provides training and technical assistance to support educators, families, and agencies in improving outcomes for children with disabilities across Pennsylvania.

Visit the PaTTAN website to explore webinars, publications, and family resources.

The National Center on Deafblindness supports an estimated 10,000 children and youth in the U.S. who are deafblind, many of whom have additional disabilities. Families can access information, technical assistance, and resources tailored to deafblindness.

Learn more at nationaldb.org.

The National Autism Center, part of the May Institute, promotes evidence-based practice in the treatment of autism spectrum disorder. The center offers reliable resources for families, practitioners, and communities on effective autism interventions.

ASERT is a statewide initiative that brings together medical centers, autism service providers, universities, and other partners to coordinate autism resources across Pennsylvania. Families can find information on services, education, and support at every age.

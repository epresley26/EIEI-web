The CACFP Initiative

The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) is a federal program that provides reimbursements for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and adults. The CACFP initiative aims to improve the quality of care in early childhood education settings by supporting healthy eating and physical activity.

What is CACFP?

The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) is a federally funded program that provides reimbursement for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and adults who are enrolled for care at participating child care centers, adult day care centers, and emergency shelters.

"CACFP plays a vital role in ensuring that children in our care receive the nutrition they need to grow, learn, and thrive."
— EIEI Nutrition Specialist

CACFP helps ensure that children and adults in participating facilities receive nutritious meals and snacks every day. The program also promotes healthy eating and physical activity through education and training.

Benefits of CACFP Participation

Community Impact
Contribute to reducing food insecurity and promoting healthy communities.

Who Can Participate?

The CACFP is available to various types of facilities that provide care for children and adults:

  • Child care centers (public and private)
  • Family child care homes
  • After-school programs
  • Emergency shelters
  • Adult day care centers
  • At-risk afterschool programs
  • Homeless shelters

Eligibility requirements vary by type of facility, but all participating programs must meet federal nutrition standards and maintain proper documentation for meal service and attendance.

How CACFP Works

Participating facilities receive reimbursements for each eligible meal and snack they serve. The reimbursement rates vary based on the type of meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack) and the economic need of the children being served.

EIEI's Role in CACFP

The Education Institute for Early Intervention (EIEI) supports childcare providers in understanding and implementing CACFP requirements. Our services include:

  • CACFP training and technical assistance
  • Menu planning and nutrition education
  • Documentation and record-keeping support
  • Staff training on food safety and nutrition
  • Assistance with CACFP application process
  • Ongoing consultation and support

Our goal is to help childcare providers successfully participate in CACFP while improving the nutritional quality of meals served to children in their care.

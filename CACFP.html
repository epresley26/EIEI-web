The CACFP Initiative

The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) is a federal program that provides reimbursements for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and adults. Through the CACFP Initiative, EIEI partners with childcare providers to strengthen healthy eating, food security, and quality in early childhood settings.

We focus especially on family and group childcare programs serving young children in under‑resourced communities.

What is CACFP?

The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) is a federally funded program that provides reimbursement for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and adults enrolled in participating child care centers, family child care homes, adult day care centers, and emergency shelters.

"CACFP plays a vital role in ensuring that children in our care receive the nutrition they need to grow, learn, and thrive."
— EIEI Nutrition Specialist

CACFP helps ensure that children and adults in participating facilities receive nutritious meals and snacks every day. The program also promotes healthy eating and physical activity through education, menu planning support, and ongoing training for providers.

Who Can Participate?

The CACFP is available to a range of facilities that provide care for children and adults, including:

  • Child care centers (public and private)
  • Family child care homes
  • After‑school and out‑of‑school time programs
  • Emergency and homeless shelters serving children
  • Adult day care centers
  • At‑risk afterschool programs

Eligibility requirements vary by facility type, but all participating programs must meet federal nutrition standards and maintain proper documentation for meal service and attendance.

How CACFP Works

Participating facilities receive reimbursements for each eligible meal and snack they serve. Reimbursement rates vary based on the type of meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack) and the economic needs of the children being served.

EIEI’s Role in CACFP

The Education Institute for Early Intervention (EIEI) partners with childcare providers to understand and implement CACFP requirements with confidence. Our support includes:

  • CACFP training and technical assistance
  • Menu planning and nutrition education
  • Documentation and record‑keeping support
  • Staff training on food safety and nutrition
  • Assistance with the CACFP application and renewal process
  • Ongoing consultation and problem‑solving support

Our goal is to help providers successfully participate in CACFP while improving the nutritional quality of meals served to children in their care.
